Research & Academic Projects

Detection of Exoplanets Using Radial Velocity Method

Analysis of Stellar Radial Velocity Curves. Developed and validated a radial velocity analysis pipeline using synthetic Keplerian models and Lomb–Scargle periodograms to detect and characterize various exoplanets. Under the guidance of Dr. Anand Narayanan, Professor and Head, Dept. of Earth and Space Sciences, IIST, Thiruvananthapuram.

Laser-Based Elephant Deterrence System

Using Bio-Acoustic and Chemical Simulation. Developed an Arduino-based device to detect and deter elephants, making use of their natural fear of bees to replace lethal methods.

Estimating the Dynamical Mass of a Galaxy Cluster

Utilizing spectroscopic data from the Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS) to identify cluster member galaxies, calculate their velocity dispersion and spatial extend, and estimate the total dynamical mass of the cluster.

Determination of Surface Tension using FIJI

Conducted a project to measure surface tension of certain liquids using the FIJI image processing software.
